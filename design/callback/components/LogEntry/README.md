One line of the Activity log: time, actor, action, reason.

**Provide** `time`, `actor`, the action as children (verb first, past tense) and `why`.

- Callback's mark is a small ink square reading CB, and its entries are set in mono. A person's mark is their initials in an italic-serif `signatory` ring, and their reason is quoted in italic serif in `signatory`.
- Every Callback entry must have a `why`.
